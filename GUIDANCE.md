## Disclaimer

> Guidance summarizes public government resources and published research for general information only. It isn't legal, safety, insurance or medical advice, may not fit your operation, and may not reflect the latest rules. Rules vary by state and city. Check the original source and talk with a qualified safety professional, your carrier or broker, or an attorney before acting on it.

The guidance describes what each source covers. It doesn't tell anyone what to do, and it isn't a complete list of what a source says or what the law requires. Federal OSHA rules don't apply in every case (for example, to some public employers or where a state runs its own OSHA plan), and state and local rules can be stricter.

## Changing the guidance

1. Edit `data/loss-prevention-guidance.json`. Each cause points to a guide id; each guide has a label (kind), its text and source ids.
2. Run `node scripts/build-guidance-md.mjs` to rebuild this page.
3. Rebuild the app (`bash build-offline.sh`).
